Every stored day's high and low gets a zone of ±{{ width }}%. Forward-only: a zone is only counted as touched by days after it formed, and a day touches it when its range overlaps the zone (trading straight through counts). The touch time is the first minute price entered the zone. Held / broke is judged on that day's close against where price came from (the previous close): a high zone hit from below breaks if the day closes above it. Inside means the previous close was already in the zone; live is today's unfinished day. Days and times in {{ day_tz }}.
