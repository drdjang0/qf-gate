Cascades

dry-run or live Backtest a cascade + New cascade

A buy ladder on one coin. Only one buy level rests at a time: the next level is placed once the previous one has bought, so a fast crash can't spend the whole ladder at once. Every bought level gets its own take-profit sell. Levels the market has already fallen through are skipped (except the last one). Set it up with % steps or type each level by hand. Dry-run: fills are simulated on Gate.io's real prices, no orders are sent.
